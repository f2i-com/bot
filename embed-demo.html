A speaking companion, embedded with one script tag.

Look at the bottom-right corner. Aria is mounted from ./embed/bot-embed.js inside an isolated Shadow DOM. She follows your cursor, speaks 24 kHz audio synthesized by tts.wasm in this tab, and answers questions from an AIML brain.

Follows the cursor

Move your mouse, or drag on a phone, anywhere on this page. Her eyes and head follow it.

200 Hz lip sync

Every vowel, lip closure (/p, b, m/) and lip-to-teeth tuck (/f, v/) comes straight from the synthesizer’s articulator data.

An AIML brain

Press Ask on the companion to try pattern matching, wildcards, memory (“My name is Alex”) and quick-reply chips. Ask “when are you open?” to hear a fact it was simply given.
